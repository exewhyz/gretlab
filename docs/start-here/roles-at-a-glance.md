title: Roles at a glance
description: Who does what in Gretlab, from platform staff to parents, and where each one starts.
---

What someone can do comes from their **roles**. Inside an organization, a role is given across the
whole organization or within one group; on the platform, staff hold platform roles. The full lists,
read from the app, are in [Roles](/reference/roles/) and [Permissions](/reference/permissions/).

The names below are a school's. Each kind uses its own words (Faculty, Mentor, Manager, Learner):
see [Kinds of organization](/start-here/kinds-of-organization/).

## Inside an organization

### Owner and admin

Run the whole organization: members, groups, roles, invites, settings and every module. **Owners**
can also make or remove other owners; every organization keeps at least one. They start at the
organization's dashboard (`/orgs/<slug>`), with the setup checklist, today's attendance, what needs
attention and shortcuts.

### Suggested roles

A new organization also starts with roles suggested for its kind: a school's Principal, Vice principal,
Class teacher, Subject teacher, Exam coordinator, Admissions officer, Office staff, Librarian, Transport
manager, Hostel warden and Counselor; a college's Dean, Head of department, Faculty advisor, Lab
assistant and Exam cell; and so on. They're ordinary roles: rename, change or delete them.

### Teacher

Teaches the groups they're given: takes registers, logs classes, writes problems, sets and grades
assignments and contests, posts notices and sees reports for those groups. Their home is **Teaching
today**: today's lessons, registers to take, to-dos (marks to enter, leave to decide) and their
classes. The Teach section (`/teach`) lists their classes across organizations.

### Staff

Someone whose roles grant something but who neither runs the organization nor teaches a class: an
office clerk who takes staff attendance, a librarian at the desk. Their home has today's check-in and a
card for each page their roles open.

### Student (member)

Sits in a class and holds no permissions. Their home is **My progress**: attendance across their
classes, work due this week, today's lessons, notices, and their bus, room and library books. They
do their work under Learn: Problems, My classes, Assignments, Contests.

### Parent

Linked to a child by the school (Parent accounts module). A parent isn't a member and holds no role:
they see only their child, read-only. Their home has a card per child (attendance, open work, bus,
the latest absences and notices) and they can ask for leave for their child.

## On the platform

The people who run the site hold platform roles: **Owner**, **Content admin**, **Content editor**,
**Reviewer**, **Support**, **Organizations manager**, **Communications** and **Auditor**, plus any
custom roles. They work in the Dashboard (`/admin`): the library, organizations, users, settings and
the activity log. Two platform permissions reach inside organizations: one to act as an organization's
admin (logged as support), one to look without changing anything.

## Where each one lands

| Who | Lands on |
| --- | --- |
| A signed-in person | `/home`: their schools, work due, contests, practice and the daily challenge |
| Someone who runs or teaches in an organization | the organization's dashboard, `/orgs/<slug>` |
| A teacher across organizations | Teach, `/teach` |
| Platform staff | Dashboard, `/admin` |
| A visitor | the public problem library, `/problems` |
