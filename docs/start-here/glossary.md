title: Glossary
description: The words Gretlab uses, in one place.
---

## Accepted

A graded submission that passed every test. Only a **Submit** can be Accepted; a Run that matches the
samples is never shown as Accepted. See [Run and Submit](/features/coding/run-and-submit/).

## Audience

Who a piece of work is for: groups, combined as conditions that must all hold. "3rd Year and CAC" is
third-year students taking CAC. An empty audience is the whole organization.

## Day off

A holiday, or a weekday outside the organization's school days (Monday to Friday unless set). Homes ask
for no registers, lessons or check-ins on a day off.

## Group

A unit of an organization's structure, named by its levels (a grade, a section, a batch, a team).
People sit in the last level; a role can be given within a group and covers every group inside it.

## Kind

What an organization is (school, college, coaching institute, cohort program, company, community,
other). Chosen once, never changed. See [Kinds of organization](/start-here/kinds-of-organization/).

## Module

A part of Gretlab an organization can switch on or off (attendance, exams, the library, the coding
lab). Limited by the kind and by the platform's policy for it.

## Organization

One school, college, company or community on Gretlab, with its own members, groups, roles and data.

## Permission

One thing a role allows, such as "Take attendance". Some have finer actions under them. See
[Permissions](/reference/permissions/).

## Platform

Gretlab itself, run by platform staff: the library, the platform's own contests and assignments, and
the organizations. "Personal" content is the platform's.

## Role

A bundle of permissions, given across an organization or within a group. See [Roles](/reference/roles/).

## Run

Trying code on the samples and your own input. Not graded and not kept in your submissions.

## Sitting

One student's single, timed attempt at a lab exam, with its own start and end.

## Submit

Grading code against every test, hidden ones included. Recorded in your history.

## Switcher

The organization switcher in the header: chooses whether lists show everything, personal (platform)
content, one organization or a mix.

## Two-factor

A second step after the password or Google: a code from an authenticator app, a code by email or a
backup code. Can be required for staff or everyone.
