## What's on it

- Published assignments' due times (with your own extension), exam windows, and peer reviews still to
  write.
- Contests you registered for, or that your organizations run.
- From each organization: its holidays, exam papers in the classes you or your children sit, your own or
  your children's leave, training you still need to do, and the dates of cohort runs you're in.

Whole-day items show as **All day** and never shift with your time zone.

## The iCal feed

Turn on a private feed and add its address to Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar. Calendar apps
can't sign in, so **the address is the key**: anyone with it sees your due dates. **New link** retires
the old one. The feed never includes marks or code, and stops for blocked accounts.

The calendar doesn't follow the header's switcher: it's your full schedule.
