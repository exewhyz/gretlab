Gretlab's features come in two halves: **the day**, switched on as modules to suit each kind of
organization, and **the lab**, the coding judge. This page maps them; each has its own page.

## Coding lab

The `coding` module: on for every kind from the start, and switchable off.

- [Problems and practice](/features/coding/problems-and-practice/): the public library, members-only
  problems, the daily challenge, lists, study plans, profiles and the leaderboard.
- [Run and Submit](/features/coding/run-and-submit/): trying code on the samples, and grading it on
  every test.
- [Assignments and the gradebook](/features/coding/assignments/): problem sets with due times, late
  penalties, rubrics, line comments, extensions and a gradebook that's always computed.
- [Quizzes and the question bank](/features/coding/quizzes/): choice, short-answer and written questions
  in assignments, exams, contests and the daily challenge.
- [Lab exams](/features/coding/lab-exams/): one timed sitting per student, limited to the lab's
  network, with the whole site locked to the exam.
- [Contests](/features/coding/contests/): ICPC or IOI scoring, a scoreboard that can freeze, and
  questions beside problems.

## Every day

- [Attendance](/features/daily/attendance/): a register per class, day and period (and course, for
  colleges), with the minimum to sit exams.
- [Timetable](/features/daily/timetable/): periods and school days, each class's week, and no teacher
  in two places at once.
- [Leave](/features/daily/leave/): students, parents and staff ask; the class teacher or the office
  decides; approved leave excuses absences.

## People

- [Members, groups and roles](/features/people/members-groups-roles/): joining, invite links, groups in
  the organization's own levels, and roles given across it or within a group.
- [Student records](/features/people/student-records/): academic years, admission numbers, enrolments,
  import and promotion.

## Assessment

- [Exams and report cards](/features/assessment/exams-and-report-cards/): term exams, marks by paper,
  the school's grading scale or a college's credits, remarks and printable report cards.
