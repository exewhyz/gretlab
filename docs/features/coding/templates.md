A template is an assignment or exam written once (problems with points and rubrics, questions, late
rules, held results and exam settings) without an address, dates or audience.

## How it works

- **Using a template** makes one ordinary assignment per class, each with its own dates, address and
  gradebook.
- **Saving a template** brings its content to every use that hasn't opened yet, keeping each use's
  dates, class, publish state, lab networks and outcome tags. Uses that have opened stay as they were.
  The save says how many it updated and kept.
- **Any assignment** can be saved as a template.
- **Platform templates** are shared with organizations like library items; an organization uses them,
  or copies one to change it.

## Step by step

1. **Coding lab › Templates › New template,** or **Save as template** from an assignment.
2. **Use** it: choose the classes and each one's dates.
