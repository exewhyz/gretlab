## What it changes

Under **Settings › Branding** an organization sets its **logo** and its **brand colour**. They
appear:

- on every page of the organization, for everyone in it: the logo at the top of the sidebar, and
  the colour on buttons, links and the current page, in place of GretLab's orange. Leaving the
  organization's pages brings GretLab's colour back;
- on its public profile;
- on the public page of every certificate it gives;
- on its PDFs: report cards and transfer certificates (the logo above the name, the colour along
  the top) and ID cards (the colour on the band, the logo at its corner).

## The logo

A PNG or JPEG of up to 2 MB; a square one works best. GIF, WebP and SVG aren't taken, because PDFs
can't hold them (and SVG can carry scripts). **Remove** goes back to your name's initials.

## The colour

Pick it or type it (`#2F6FDE`). It's previewed on a button and a link on both the light and dark
page, and has to show up on both: a colour too pale for the light page or too dark for dark mode
is refused, with which shade to choose instead. Text on the colour is white or dark, whichever
reads better. **Use GretLab's colour** goes back to the orange.

## Edge cases

- Changes to the logo and colour are in the activity log.
- Documents printed before a change keep what they had; printing again uses the new branding.
