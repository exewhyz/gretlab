A holiday is a name and a first and last day (at most three months).

## What a holiday does

- It's on everyone's [calendar](/features/communication/calendar/) and iCal feed.
- Homes ask for no registers, lessons or check-ins, and the greeting names it.
- With the organization's school days (Timetable), it makes up a [day off](/start-here/glossary/#day-off).

## Step by step

1. **Every day › Holidays:** give it a name and its days, and add it. It needs organization-wide
   "Organization settings".
2. Remove one from the list. Adding and removing are in the activity log.
